💡 How StudentHub works

StudentHub is built with 100% offline, privacy-first technology. All your schedules, expenses, attendance, and tasks are stored locally in your browser via localStorage. No servers, no tracking, and zero internet connection required after opening.

📚 Exam Planner

Distributes unfinished syllabus topics evenly across available calendar days before each exam.

topicsPerDay = ceil(unfinished / daysAvailable)

💰 Budget Tracker

Calculates daily expenditure capacity to ensure your monthly allowance lasts through the month.

safePerDay = floor(remaining / daysLeftInMonth)

✅ Attendance Safe Skips

Computes how many consecutive classes you can safely miss without dropping below required cutoff.

bunkable = floor(present * 100 / required - total)

🎯 Attendance Recovery

Calculates minimum consecutive classes needed to raise attendance back above the cutoff.

needed = ceil((req * tot - 100 * pres) / (100 - req))

📝 Task Priority Sorting

Orders tasks in a two-tier hierarchy: High > Medium > Low priority, followed by earliest due date.

priorityWeight (3 > 2 > 1) then dueAsc

⏱️ Pomodoro Timer

Standardized 25m Focus / 5m Break cycle with browser Web Audio API chimes and daily counters.

step = floor(sec / 60) : (sec % 60)
